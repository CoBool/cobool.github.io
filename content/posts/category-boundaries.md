Categories are useful only if they stay broad.

The first set is intentionally small: build log, engineering, notes, and workflow.

## Boundary examples

| Post idea | Category | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adding a Markdown renderer | `build-log` | It records project progress |
| Choosing frontmatter defaults | `engineering` | It defines a contract |
| Comparing surface styles | `notes` | It captures design thinking |
| PR review checklist | `workflow` | It improves process |

When a post seems to fit two categories, choose the one that best explains why the post exists.

Checklist before adding a new category:

- [ ] At least five existing or planned posts need it
- [ ] It is broader than a tag
- [ ] It would help a reader browse the archive

Until those are true, a tag is probably enough.
